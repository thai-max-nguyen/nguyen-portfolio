    How a service cycle runs

    A typical client case

    Question
    A dairy brand in Vietnam saw its share dip in one region and asked whether the data was right.
    Approach
    I checked store coverage and product coding with Operations, then split sales by channel and pack size.
    Insight
    The data was sound. The dip came from one pack size in traditional trade, where a competitor was running promotions.
    Recommendation
    Review promotion timing and pack-size mix for traditional trade in that region.
    Impact
    The client adjusted its next-quarter promotion plan, and share held steady in the following data reads.

      How I resolve a client data query

      1. Understand the question. Which number, which period, which market view.
      2. Reproduce it. Pull the same cut in the NIQ platform and compare it with the client’s figure.
      3. Find the cause. Check coverage, product coding and restatements with Operations or Data Science.
      4. Reply plainly. Give the answer, the cause and what it means for their numbers.
      5. Log and learn. Record the query and the fix, and share repeat patterns with the team.
